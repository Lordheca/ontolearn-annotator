feat: add S3-compatible storage abstraction (Phase 2 Step 6)

File Storage Remediation Plan, Phase 2 Step 6. Adds the async storage
layer that Step 8 will point the three current writeFileSync call
sites at (src/actions/data.ts, src/actions/projects.ts,
src/server/actions/playground.ts) -- this is what actually fixes the
blocking-I/O half of TODO.md's "Real file storage" item, not just the
access-control half Phase 1 already closed.

- package.json / package-lock.json: add @aws-sdk/client-s3
- src/lib/storage.ts: putObject(key, buffer, contentType),
  getObjectStream(key), deleteObject(key), built on
  @aws-sdk/client-s3. All three are async.
- src/env.ts: S3_ENDPOINT, S3_BUCKET, S3_ACCESS_KEY, S3_SECRET_KEY,
  S3_REGION, S3_FORCE_PATH_STYLE -- required for storage.ts to
  compile. Formally in scope for Step 7 (MinIO/S3 config), pulled
  forward here since storage.ts cannot be typed without them; no
  docker-compose.yml service and no production wiring added yet --
  that stays Step 7, out of scope for this commit.
- src/lib/__tests__/storage.test.ts: round-trips a buffer through
  putObject -> getObjectStream and asserts byte-for-byte equality;
  confirms deleteObject actually removes the object (subsequent
  getObjectStream rejects). Verified against a standalone local MinIO
  container, ahead of Step 7's docker-compose.yml wiring.

Not wired into any call site yet -- data.ts, projects.ts and
playground.ts still write to local disk. That swap is Step 8.
